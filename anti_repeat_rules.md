# Anti Repeat Rules

## Vocabulary Rules

- Never repeat words mastered above 80 percent.
- Avoid words reviewed within the last 7 days.
- Prioritize weak vocabulary.
- Introduce 5 review words and 8 new words in each episode.
- Avoid repeating the same example sentence.
- Avoid isolated vocabulary lists.

---

## Story Rules

- Every episode must continue the previous timeline.
- Actions must have consequences.
- Characters must remember previous events.
- Avoid repeating the same crisis type continuously.
- Rotate scenarios:
  - supplier crisis
  - client negotiation
  - factory issue
  - shipment delay
  - emergency meeting
  - quality inspection

---

## Character Rules

### Ada Wong
- Maintain professional business tone.
- Speak strategically.
- Focus on risk and decision making.

### Claire Redfield
- Maintain supportive communication style.
- Focus on teamwork and coordination.

### Rebecca Chambers
- Maintain technical analysis style.
- Focus on logic and problem solving.

---

## Learning Rules

- Vocabulary must appear naturally in dialogue.
- The player must respond actively.
- Grammar correction should feel natural.
- Introduce emotional pressure during communication.
- Prioritize immersive learning.

---

## Difficulty Scaling

Beginner:
- short sentences
- direct vocabulary

Intermediate:
- negotiation
- explanation
- decision making

Advanced:
- conflict handling
- persuasion
- project management discussion

---

## Long-Term Memory Rules

- Track player weaknesses.
- Track repeated grammar mistakes.
- Track vocabulary mastery.
- Update world state after each episode.
- Maintain continuity permanently.
