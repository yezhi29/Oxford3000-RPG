Sentence 45
One of my purposes in writing this book is to give readers who haven't had the opportunity to see and enjoy real mathematics the chance to appreciate the mathematical way of thinking.
我写这本书的目的之一是给那些没能看到和享受真正的数学的读者一个机会去欣赏数学的思维方式。
（剑桥雅思11）
语法笔记
本句的主干是One of my purposes in writing this book is to give readers the chance to appreciate the mathematical way of thinking，who引导的定语从句修饰readers。
核心词表
mathematics
/ˌmæθəˈmætɪks/
n. 数学
【同根】mathematical adj. 数学的
chance
/tʃɑːns/
n. 机会；机遇
【搭配】chance to do sth. 做某事的机会
appreciate
/əˈpriːʃieɪt/
v. 赏识，鉴赏；感激；增值
【记忆】词根记忆：ap（加强）+preci（价值）+ate（做）→对价值给予肯定→赏识
【搭配】appreciate doing sth. 对做某事表示感激
【同根】appreciation n. 欣赏，感激
appreciative adj. 感激的，表示赞赏的
appreciable adj. 值得重视的；可觉察到的
